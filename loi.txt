**DocFlow Offline – All-in-One Document Viewer, Editor & Converter**

Manage your documents anytime, anywhere — even without an internet connection!

DocFlow Offline is a convenient document management app that lets you view, edit, organize, and convert Word, Excel, PDF, and other common file formats directly on your Android device.

Whether you're working, studying, or managing personal documents, DocFlow Offline puts essential office tools in one easy-to-use app.

**KEY FEATURES**

📄 **Word Document Tools**
- Open and read DOCX documents.
- Create and edit documents.
- Format text with basic editing tools.
- Search and replace text.
- Export supported document content to PDF.

📊 **Excel Spreadsheet Tools**
- Open, create, and edit XLSX spreadsheets.
- Work with multiple worksheets.
- Use common formulas and calculation functions.
- Format cells, adjust colors, and merge cells.
- Zoom in or out for easier spreadsheet navigation.
- Save edited spreadsheets and export to PDF.

📕 **PDF Tools**
- Open and read PDF documents.
- Zoom and navigate through pages.
- Search and select text in supported PDFs.
- Merge multiple PDF files.
- Extract selected PDF pages.
- Convert PDF pages to PNG images.
- Create PDF documents from images.

🔄 **Document Conversion**
- Word to PDF.
- Excel to PDF.
- Text-based PDF to Word.
- Text-based PDF to Excel.
- Images to PDF.
- PDF to PNG.

📁 **Easy File Management**
- Browse files by document type.
- Quickly access recently opened documents.
- Organize and manage your documents.
- Save and share files using Android's file tools.

🔒 **100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE**

No internet connection required for document processing.

Your documents are processed locally on your device without being uploaded to external servers.

Enjoy a convenient, private document workflow wherever you are.

✨ **SIMPLE & USER-FRIENDLY**

DocFlow Offline features a clean interface, easy navigation, and practical tools designed for everyday document tasks.

**IMPORTANT NOTES**

Some advanced formatting, complex layouts, and embedded elements may not be fully preserved during conversion.

Text extraction requires a PDF with selectable text. OCR for scanned documents and images is not currently supported.

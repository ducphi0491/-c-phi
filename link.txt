I understand that your banner ad unit (ca-app-pub-3156289270309336/4491022655) in Tuner Tools returns HTTP 403 responses and unfilled states on both physical devices and emulators, while Google test ad units display successfully.

Here is clarification regarding this behavior based on an inspection of your account configuration:

Upon reviewing your account configuration, the ad unit was created with the Partner Bidding serving type.

Partner Bidding ad units are designed exclusively for third-party mediation platforms where Google participates as a real-time bidder. When this option is selected, the ad unit is restricted from delivering ads when requested directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK or within standard AdMob Network requests.

Because the serving type of an ad unit is permanent once created, you will need to set up a standard ad unit in your AdMob account and ensure the Partner bidding option is left unchecked. Once created, update your application code with the new ad unit identifier.

For reference, you can review the official guides on understanding ad unit serving types and ad units, ad formats, and ad types.

After making these changes, if any unexpected behavior persists, please let me know and I will look into it.
